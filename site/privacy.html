calmdoo

Privacy

Effective 18 October 2026. This covers the calmdoo app for Mac and the website calmdoo.com.

The short version

  • The app changes other apps' sound in memory and plays it straight back. It never records it, saves it or sends it anywhere.
  • The app has no internet code at all. Your settings stay on your Mac.
  • The website has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Its host sees your IP address in order to deliver the page.

The Mac app

Sound from other apps

To change another app's volume, macOS has calmdoo pass that app's sound through itself. That is why macOS asks you once for the System Audio Recording permission. calmdoo uses it only for apps whose volume, EQ or compressor you have changed, or for every app while "Boost everything" is above 100%.

The sound is adjusted in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, and played straight back. It is never written to disk and never leaves your Mac. You can take the permission away at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.

Settings saved on your Mac

calmdoo remembers your choices: each app's volume, mute, EQ and compressor, and the "Boost everything" level. They are saved only for apps whose settings you have changed, under each app's identifier (for example com.spotify.client). So this file lists the apps whose sound you have adjusted. It is stored at ~/Library/Preferences/com.calmdoo.app.plist and is never sent anywhere.

Problem log

If something goes wrong, for example macOS refuses the permission or an app's sound cannot be changed, calmdoo writes one line about it to ~/Library/Logs/calmdoo.log, including the name of the app involved. The log holds problems only, never a history of what you played, is limited to 1 MB, and is never sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

calmdoo contains no networking code. It has no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, no accounts, no ads and no update checks. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.

macOS itself may send diagnostics to Apple if you have allowed that in System Settings. That is between you and Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Removing everything

Quit calmdoo, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete ~/Library/Preferences/com.calmdoo.app.plist and ~/Library/Logs/calmdoo.log. To clear its permission too, remove calmdoo in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
